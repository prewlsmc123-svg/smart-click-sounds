# Smart Click Sounds

Real click sounds on every jump, that react to **how** you click:

- **Spam sounds like spam.** Clicks 150 ms+ apart are full strength; really fast clicks get lighter, like a real hand. Loudness never jumps from quiet straight to loud.
- **Every press plays with its own release**, recorded from the same physical click.
- **No repeats.** Every sound is used once before any repeats, never one of the last 32 again, plus tiny pitch/volume variation on each click.
- **Always in sync.** The game plays the sounds itself, so it lines up with you, a macro, practice mode or speedhack.

Uses your GDClickbot pack (`Documents\GDClickbot\clickpack`) if you have one, otherwise the pack built in. Any clickpack folder works too (see settings).

## Macro player

Plays **xdBot / Mega Hack `.gdr`** and **Mega Hack `.gdr2`** macros directly, no converting. Pick the file in settings (Macro file), then press **F7** (or turn on *Play the macro*). The level restarts and the macro plays, with these click sounds. xdBot macros use their saved positions to stay exactly on track, just like in xdBot. **F6** opens the settings.
